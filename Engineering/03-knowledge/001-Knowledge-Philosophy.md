# Purpose

This document defines the philosophy, lifecycle, governance, and evolution of knowledge within the Work Intelligence Platform (WIP).

It establishes the principles that determine how knowledge is acquired, validated, governed, represented, consumed, and continuously improved.

Knowledge is the foundational asset of WIP.

Everything else exists to create, manage, reason upon, or execute against knowledge.

---

# Philosophy Statement

WIP exists to transform evidence into governed knowledge, governed knowledge into intelligence, intelligence into execution, and execution into continuous organizational improvement.

Knowledge is not static.

Knowledge continuously evolves through validated learning.

---

# Knowledge Hierarchy

Knowledge within WIP follows the following hierarchy.

```text
Evidence
    ↓
Information
    ↓
Knowledge
    ↓
Understanding
    ↓
Intelligence
    ↓
Decision
    ↓
Execution
    ↓
Outcome
    ↓
Learning
    ↓
Improved Evidence
```

The lifecycle never terminates.

Every execution produces additional evidence that continuously improves organizational knowledge.

---

# Definitions

## Evidence

Evidence is a verifiable observation originating from an authoritative source.

Examples include:

* PMBOK guidance
* ISO standards
* Regulatory publications
* Customer interviews
* Market reports
* Operational metrics
* System telemetry
* Research publications
* Approved enterprise documents

Evidence is always attributable.

---

## Information

Information is structured evidence with context.

Information answers:

* What happened?
* Where?
* When?
* Who?
* Under what conditions?

Information alone does not explain meaning.

---

## Knowledge

Knowledge is validated information combined with contextual understanding.

Knowledge explains:

* Why something happens
* Relationships
* Dependencies
* Constraints
* Business meaning

Knowledge is governed.

---

## Understanding

Understanding is the ability to interpret knowledge within a specific business context.

Different organizations may interpret the same knowledge differently while referencing identical evidence.

---

## Intelligence

Intelligence is the ability to reason across multiple knowledge sources to produce explainable recommendations, predictions, insights, and decisions.

Artificial Intelligence within WIP consumes governed knowledge rather than raw data.

Knowledge may originate from multiple authoritative sources including:

* PMBOK
* Future PMBOK editions
* IPMA
* PRINCE2
* ISO Standards
* Agile frameworks
* Lean
* Six Sigma
* TOGAF
* Enterprise documents
* Customer Intelligence (CuIP)
* Competitor Intelligence (CIP)
* Market Intelligence
* Technology Intelligence
* Regulatory Intelligence
* Operational telemetry

No single knowledge source is considered universally authoritative.

Knowledge is governed through evidence and validation.

# Knowledge Lifecycle

Every knowledge asset follows the same lifecycle.

```text
Acquire
    ↓
Validate
    ↓
Govern
    ↓
Version
    ↓
Publish
    ↓
Reason
    ↓
Execute
    ↓
Observe
    ↓
Improve
```

Learning continuously feeds the next acquisition cycle.

---

# Knowledge Governance

Every knowledge asset shall contain:

* Unique Identifier
* Source
* Source Version
* Date Acquired
* Validation Status
* Approval Status
* Confidence Score
* Owner
* Domain
* Relationships
* Effective Date
* Review Date

Knowledge without governance is considered untrusted.

---

# Knowledge Quality Principles

Every knowledge asset must satisfy:

* Accuracy
* Completeness
* Traceability
* Explainability
* Versioning
* Consistency
* Context
* Evidence
* Reviewability
* Reusability

---

# Knowledge Versioning

Knowledge is never overwritten.

Knowledge evolves through versioning.

Historical knowledge remains available for:

* Auditing
* AI explainability
* Historical reasoning
* Regulatory compliance
* Enterprise learning

---

# Knowledge Evolution

WIP is designed as a self-evolving knowledge platform.

New standards, methodologies, technologies, regulations, market changes, customer behaviors, and enterprise learning continuously enrich the knowledge base.

Evolution occurs through governance rather than replacement.

---

# Relationship with AI

Artificial Intelligence does not create authoritative knowledge.

Artificial Intelligence reasons over governed knowledge.

AI recommendations must reference:

* Evidence
* Knowledge sources
* Reasoning path
* Confidence
* Governing framework

Explainability is mandatory.

---

# Relationship with the Enterprise Ontology

The Enterprise Ontology provides semantic structure.

The Knowledge Philosophy provides epistemological rules.

The ontology defines what exists.

The philosophy defines why knowledge can be trusted.

---

# Relationship with the Knowledge Graph

The Knowledge Graph represents governed knowledge through entities and relationships.

The Knowledge Philosophy governs the quality and validity of those relationships.

---

# Engineering Implications

Every future WIP capability must comply with this philosophy including:

* CIP
* CuIP
* Enterprise Ontology
* Knowledge Graph
* Reasoning Engine
* Workflow Engine
* AI Agents
* Platform Services
* Enterprise Applications

Knowledge remains the enduring enterprise asset.

Applications are consumers of knowledge.

---

# Closing Principle

The competitive advantage of WIP is not software.

The competitive advantage of WIP is continuously governed enterprise knowledge that evolves through evidence, learning, and human oversight.

Software delivers the experience.

Knowledge delivers the intelligence.
