# Purpose

The Blueprint Management System (BMS) defines how every artifact within the WIP Blueprint is created, identified, governed, reviewed, versioned, approved, related, and evolved.

It establishes a single operating standard for managing the company's intellectual assets throughout their lifecycle.

The BMS governs the blueprint itself.

It does not govern the software platform.

---

# Scope

The Blueprint Management System applies to every artifact produced within WIP, including but not limited to:

* Strategy
* Research
* Knowledge
* Architecture
* Platform
* Products
* Engineering
* Operations
* Governance
* Artificial Intelligence
* Documentation
* Standards
* Decisions

No artifact exists outside the Blueprint Management System.

---

# Objectives

The Blueprint Management System shall:

* Maintain a single source of truth.
* Ensure complete traceability.
* Preserve evidence for every significant decision.
* Standardize artifact structure.
* Support continuous evolution.
* Enable AI navigation of the blueprint.
* Prevent duplication.
* Maintain governance throughout the lifecycle.

---

# Blueprint Structure

The WIP Blueprint consists of the following domains:

* Constitution
* Strategy
* Research
* Knowledge
* Architecture
* Platform
* Products
* Engineering
* Operations
* Governance

Each artifact belongs to one and only one primary domain.

Cross-domain relationships are represented through traceability.

---

# Artifact Lifecycle

Every artifact progresses through the following lifecycle.

```text
Draft
    ↓
Review
    ↓
Approved
    ↓
Active
    ↓
Superseded
    ↓
Archived
```

Artifacts are never deleted.

They remain available for audit, learning, historical reasoning, and traceability.

---

# Artifact Ownership

Every artifact shall have one accountable owner.

The owner is responsible for:

* Accuracy
* Review
* Versioning
* Traceability
* Evidence quality
* Approval requests

Ownership may change over time through governance.

---

# Versioning

Every artifact shall be version controlled.

Major versions indicate structural changes.

Minor versions indicate functional improvements.

Patch versions indicate corrections.

Historical versions remain permanently accessible.

---

# Review Policy

Every artifact shall define:

* Review frequency
* Next review date
* Trigger events requiring review
* Responsible reviewer

Reviews may also be triggered by:

* New evidence
* New standards
* Technology changes
* Market changes
* Regulatory updates
* Architectural decisions

---

# Evidence Chain

Every artifact shall answer the following questions:

* Why does this artifact exist?
* What evidence justified its creation?
* Which decision does it support?
* Which parent artifact governs it?
* Which child artifacts depend upon it?
* What value does it produce?
* When should it be reviewed?

These answers form the Evidence Chain.

---

# Dependency Management

Every artifact shall explicitly identify:

* Parent artifacts
* Child artifacts
* Dependencies
* Referenced artifacts
* Referencing artifacts

Impact analysis shall be possible before any significant modification.

---

# AI Compatibility

The Blueprint Management System shall support AI-assisted navigation.

Every artifact must be structured so that AI can determine:

* Purpose
* Context
* Dependencies
* Governance
* Relationships
* Version
* Evidence
* Status

The blueprint must be machine-readable as well as human-readable.

---

# Governance Rules

The following rules are mandatory:

* Every artifact shall have a unique identifier.
* Every artifact shall have an owner.
* Every artifact shall define its lifecycle status.
* Every artifact shall contain version information.
* Every artifact shall maintain traceability.
* Every artifact shall identify supporting evidence where applicable.
* Every artifact shall participate in the Evidence Chain.

---

# Success Criteria

The Blueprint Management System is successful when:

* Every artifact is uniquely identifiable.
* Every artifact is traceable.
* Every decision is explainable.
* Every dependency is discoverable.
* Every change is governed.
* Every stakeholder can navigate the blueprint with confidence.
* AI agents can reason across the blueprint without ambiguity.

---

# Guiding Principle

The Blueprint is a living representation of the WIP company.

Its integrity depends on disciplined governance, evidence-based evolution, and complete traceability.

The Blueprint Management System exists to preserve that integrity throughout the lifetime of WIP.
