# WIP Constitution

Version: 1.0.0

Document ID: WIP-CON-000

Status: Active

Owner: WIP Leadership

Approved By: CEO & CTO

---

# Preamble

The Work Intelligence Platform (WIP) exists to improve the way organizations think, decide, execute, learn, and evolve.

WIP is founded on the belief that better decisions are made when governed knowledge, verifiable evidence, human expertise, and cognitive technology work together as one integrated system.

This Constitution establishes the enduring principles that govern every decision made within WIP. These principles are intended to remain stable as technologies, standards, methodologies, markets, and organizations evolve.

Every strategic, product, engineering, knowledge, and operational decision shall align with this Constitution.

---

# Article I — Purpose

The purpose of WIP is to build an Enterprise Cognitive Platform that enables organizations to transform evidence into trusted knowledge, knowledge into intelligence, intelligence into execution, and execution into continuous improvement.

---

# Article II — Vision

WIP shall become the most trusted Enterprise Cognitive Platform by enabling organizations to continuously acquire, govern, reason upon, and apply knowledge through software that remains explainable, evidence-based, and human-centered.

---

# Article III — Mission

Design and operate a platform where knowledge and software coexist as equal pillars, continuously strengthening one another to solve complex organizational problems through governed intelligence.

---

# Article IV — Constitutional Principles

The following principles are immutable unless formally amended through constitutional governance.

## CP-001 — Knowledge and Software are Equal

Knowledge gives meaning.

Software enables execution.

Neither is subordinate to the other.

The value of WIP emerges from the continuous interaction between both.

---

## CP-002 — Evidence Before Assertion

Claims, recommendations, automation, and decisions must be supported by verifiable evidence or explicitly identified as assumptions.

---

## CP-003 — Human-Centered Intelligence

Artificial Intelligence augments human capability.

Governance, accountability, and strategic ownership remain with people.

---

## CP-004 — Continuous Evolution

WIP is intentionally designed to evolve.

New knowledge, standards, technologies, methodologies, regulations, and market insights shall be incorporated through governed evolution rather than disruptive redesign.

---

## CP-005 — Governance by Design

Governance is a foundational capability of the platform.

Every knowledge asset, AI recommendation, workflow, and architectural decision shall be traceable, versioned, reviewable, and explainable.

---

## CP-006 — Open to Standards

WIP is designed to support multiple authoritative frameworks.

PMBOK 7 is the initial governing framework because it aligns with the founding vision of WIP.

Future frameworks and standards shall coexist through governed versioning rather than replacement.

---

## CP-007 — Simplicity for Humans

The platform shall present a simple, intuitive user experience while hiding the complexity of the underlying cognitive platform.

Complexity belongs in the architecture, not in the user experience.

---

## CP-008 — Self-Improvement

Every interaction with WIP has the potential to improve WIP.

Learning is a permanent capability of the platform.

---

# Article V — Strategic Assets

The enduring strategic assets of WIP are:

* Governed Knowledge
* Evidence
* Enterprise Intelligence
* Cognitive Models
* Platform Architecture
* Trust

Applications are important, but they are consumers of these assets rather than the assets themselves.

---

# Article VI — Decision Framework

Every significant decision within WIP shall answer the following questions:

1. What problem is being solved?
2. What evidence supports this decision?
3. What knowledge is required?
4. How is the reasoning explained?
5. How will success be measured?
6. How will learning improve future decisions?

---

# Article VII — Evolution Policy

WIP shall continuously observe changes in:

* Standards
* Technology
* Markets
* Customers
* Competitors
* Regulations
* Organizational practices

Validated knowledge shall be incorporated through governed evolution.

Existing knowledge shall be versioned rather than destroyed.

---

# Article VIII — Success Definition

WIP succeeds when organizations consistently make better decisions, execute with greater confidence, and continuously improve through trusted knowledge, evidence, software, and human judgment working together.

---

# Closing Statement

The Constitution is the highest governing document of the Work Intelligence Platform.

Every roadmap, architecture, product, engineering decision, AI capability, workflow, and application shall derive from and remain consistent with these constitutional principles.

When uncertainty exists, the Constitution shall take precedence.
