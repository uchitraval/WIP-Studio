# Purpose

The Enterprise Ontology defines the canonical semantic model of the Work Intelligence Platform (WIP).

It establishes the common language, concepts, relationships, constraints, and governance rules that enable every platform capability to understand enterprise knowledge consistently.

The ontology provides semantic interoperability across knowledge sources, artificial intelligence, platform services, workflows, APIs, analytics, and applications.

---

# Objectives

The ontology shall:

* Define a shared enterprise vocabulary.
* Standardize semantic meaning.
* Eliminate ambiguity.
* Support enterprise-scale knowledge representation.
* Enable explainable AI reasoning.
* Provide the foundation for the Enterprise Knowledge Graph.
* Support continuous evolution through governed extensions.

# Semantic Constraints

Every concept shall define:

* Identifier
* Name
* Description
* Attributes
* Relationships
* Constraints
* Lifecycle
* Owner
* Version

No concept may exist without governance.

---

# Ontology Governance

Changes require:

* Evidence
* Impact Assessment
* Architecture Review
* Version Update
* Approval

Breaking semantic changes shall require a major version increment.

---

# Version Strategy

The ontology shall evolve through additive change whenever possible.

Existing concepts shall remain stable.

Deprecated concepts remain available until formally retired through governance.

---

# Knowledge Source Mapping

Every acquired knowledge source shall map its concepts to the ontology.

Examples include:

* PMBOK
* IPMA
* PRINCE2
* ISO
* CIP
* CuIP
* Enterprise Policies
* Internal Standards

This enables semantic interoperability across heterogeneous knowledge sources.

---

# Knowledge Graph Integration

The ontology defines the schema of the Enterprise Knowledge Graph.

The Knowledge Graph stores instances and relationships.

The ontology defines their meaning.

---

# AI Integration

Artificial Intelligence shall reason over ontology-governed concepts.

AI recommendations shall reference ontology concepts rather than proprietary internal structures.

This ensures explainability, consistency, and interoperability.
