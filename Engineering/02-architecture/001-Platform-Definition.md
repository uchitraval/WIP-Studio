# Purpose

This document defines the identity, purpose, boundaries, and long-term direction of the Work Intelligence Platform (WIP).

It is the authoritative definition of what WIP is, what it is designed to achieve, and the architectural principles that govern its evolution.

Every engineering, product, and AI decision must align with this document.

---

# Platform Definition

WIP (Work Intelligence Platform) is an AI-native Enterprise Intelligence Platform that models, understands, builds, operates, and continuously improves organizations through a unified knowledge and execution architecture.

WIP is not a collection of business applications.

WIP is a platform upon which enterprise applications are composed from shared capabilities, enterprise knowledge, workflows, and artificial intelligence.

---

# Mission

Enable every organization to build, operate, and evolve its business from a single intelligent platform.

---

# Vision

Create the world's most intelligent enterprise platform where people, processes, knowledge, AI, and data work as one connected system.

---

- Platform before Applications
- Knowledge before Data
- AI Native
- Everything is an Entity
- Everything is Connected
- API First
- Event Driven
- Cloud Native
- Security by Design
- Explainable Intelligence

---

# What WIP Is

WIP is:

- An Enterprise Intelligence Platform
- A Knowledge-Centric Platform
- An AI-Native Platform
- A Workflow Platform
- A Platform Engineering System
- A Business Operating Platform
- A Foundation for Enterprise Applications

---

# What WIP Is Not

WIP is not:

- A Project Management Tool
- A CRM
- An ERP
- A Task Manager
- A Note-Taking Application
- A Collection of Independent Modules

These may exist as applications built on WIP, but they are not WIP itself.

---

# Primary Users

The platform is designed for:

- Business Leaders
- Operations Teams
- Project Teams
- Product Teams
- Engineering Teams
- Enterprise Architects
- AI Agents
- System Integrators

---

# Architectural Boundaries

The platform owns:

- Enterprise Knowledge
- Enterprise Entities
- Enterprise Relationships
- Enterprise Workflows
- Enterprise Intelligence
- Enterprise APIs
- Enterprise Security
- Enterprise Automation

Business applications consume these capabilities rather than implementing them independently.

---

# Long-Term Goal

Become the operating system for modern enterprises by providing a single platform that can understand, coordinate, automate, and continuously improve organizational work.

---

# Success Definition

WIP succeeds when new enterprise applications can be created by composing existing platform capabilities instead of building new systems from scratch.
