# Purpose

The PMBOK Intelligence Program defines how the Work Intelligence Platform (WIP) acquires, governs, structures, versions, and operationalizes knowledge from the Project Management Body of Knowledge (PMBOK).

The objective is not to reproduce PMBOK, but to transform its concepts into governed enterprise knowledge that can be understood by software, reasoned upon by AI, and applied by organizations.

---

# Objectives

The program shall:

* Establish PMBOK as the first reference knowledge framework.
* Create a repeatable knowledge acquisition process.
* Validate the WIP knowledge architecture.
* Support future PMBOK editions through governed versioning.
* Demonstrate explainable AI reasoning using standards-based knowledge.

---

# Scope

The program includes:

* PMBOK Principles
* Performance Domains
* Models
* Methods
* Artifacts
* Tailoring Guidance
* Glossary Concepts
* Relationships
* Version History

The program excludes copyrighted reproduction of PMBOK content.

Only governed representations, metadata, relationships, and derived enterprise knowledge shall be stored.

---

# Knowledge Acquisition Lifecycle

PMBOK knowledge shall follow the standard WIP lifecycle:

1. Acquire
2. Classify
3. Validate
4. Govern
5. Model
6. Version
7. Publish
8. Reason
9. Improve

---

# Knowledge Decomposition

Every PMBOK concept shall be decomposed into structured knowledge units.

Each knowledge unit shall contain:

* Identifier
* Name
* Description
* Category
* Relationships
* Source Reference
* Version
* Confidence
* Evidence
* Status

Knowledge units become the smallest governed elements within WIP.

---

# Relationship Mapping

Knowledge units shall be connected through explicit relationships, including:

* Depends On
* Influences
* Related To
* Supersedes
* Tailors
* Enables
* Constrains

These relationships will populate the Enterprise Knowledge Graph.

---

# Version Strategy

PMBOK versions shall coexist.

The platform shall never overwrite an existing version.

Each version shall remain independently governable and traceable.

Future editions shall extend the knowledge model without breaking existing implementations.

---

# AI Consumption

AI services shall consume governed knowledge units rather than raw PMBOK text.

Recommendations generated by AI shall reference:

* Governing knowledge units
* Evidence
* Framework version
* Confidence
* Reasoning path

---

# Success Metrics

The PMBOK Intelligence Program is successful when:

* PMBOK concepts are represented as governed knowledge.
* Knowledge relationships are established.
* AI can reason over the knowledge model.
* Future PMBOK editions can be incorporated without redesign.
* The approach is reusable for additional standards.

---

# Future Expansion

The architecture established through this program shall become the template for:

* IPMA Intelligence Program
* PRINCE2 Intelligence Program
* ISO Intelligence Program
* Agile Intelligence Program
* Internal Organizational Standards

---

# Guiding Principle

PMBOK is the first knowledge source, not the only one.

The long-term objective is to build a standards-agnostic enterprise knowledge platform capable of continuously evolving through governed acquisition and evidence-based intelligence.
